# amandinah

A birthday surprise for Amanda (Amandinah), 15 October.

`index.html` is the whole page, a stack of instant-photo cards she swipes through:

1. Five photo cards (her selfie, the swing, the pacifier, the balcony, the mirror).
2. A volleyball card that opens a full-screen beach game: keep the ball up for 15 touches.
3. A cake card with the blue cloud cake photo: tap the cake to make a wish, or play the song.
4. Saud's message.

`photos/` holds the photos the page uses, and `audio/happy-birthday.mp3` is the birthday song.

To put it online, enable GitHub Pages for this repo (Settings > Pages > deploy from the branch, root folder).
