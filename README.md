# amandinah

A birthday gift for Amanda (Amandinah), 15 October.

`index.html` is the whole site: a fashion-magazine "birthday issue" that opens from a wrapped gift into story pages she taps through:

1. Cover
2. Capítulo 1: baby photo on the swing
3. Antes & agora spread
4. Capítulo 2: mirror selfie
5. Perfil: Libra, vôlei, a praia, Periquito
6. Entrevista with Periquito (tap to hear him)
7. Bolo: play "Parabéns pra Você" and blow out the candles
8. Volleyball challenge: 15 touches for the 15th
9. Carta do editor from Saud
10. Finale with confetti

`photos/` holds the photos the page uses.

To put it online, enable GitHub Pages for this repo (Settings > Pages > deploy from the branch, root folder).
